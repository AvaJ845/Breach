Breach Kit Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Breach Kit is built to be private by default. This policy describes what the app does — and does not — collect.

No account

Breach Kit does not require an account and does not operate a user database.

Data stored on your device

  • Claim status, notes, and watched settlements
  • Optional email addresses you choose to save for rescanning
  • Notification preference for deadline reminders

This information stays in on-device storage (UserDefaults). It is not uploaded to our servers — we do not run an app backend for Breach Kit.

Email scans

When you use Scan, matching runs on-device against Breach Kit’s curated catalog. Your email address is not transmitted for scanning.

Notifications

If you enable deadline reminders, Breach Kit schedules local notifications via Apple’s UserNotifications framework. Notification content is generated on-device.

Tracking & analytics

Breach Kit does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Links to claim sites

Opening an official claim site uses Safari (or your default browser). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions: use the support email listed on the App Store product page.
