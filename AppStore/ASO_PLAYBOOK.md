# Breach Kit — ASO Playbook (applied)

The Habit Kit / Focus Kit developer drives ~$50K/mo with **98% organic App Store
search and $0 ads.** His method is a three-part engine feeding a flywheel:
**Discovery → Conversion → Momentum.** Below is that method applied to Breach Kit.

---

## Naming decision (Indie Battlefield)

Fight for high-volume keywords, not ghost-town niches:

| Candidate | Why |
|---|---|
| **Breach Tracker - Breach Kit** ✅ | Mirrors `Habit Tracker - Habit Kit`. Primary keyword first, Kit brand second. Home Screen = `Breach Kit`. |
| Claim Kit | Strong payout intent, weaker privacy/North Star signal |
| Privacy Kit | Soft — low claim intent |

**Subtitle (no repeats):** `Settlements & class actions`
**Hidden keywords:** claim,payout,privacy,identity,leak,hack,monitor,scan,exposed,compensation,alert,lawsuit

---

## ① Discovery (keywords) — *get found*
Full strings live in **`METADATA.md`**.

Because Apple indexes Name + Subtitle + Keywords as **one string**, unique terms
combine into phrases: `breach tracker`, `class action claims`, `settlement alert`,
`data breach monitor`, `identity leak scan`.

---

## ② Conversion (screenshots) — *get downloaded*
**3–5 seconds.** Authentic functional UI beats polished abstraction.

Recommended order:
1. **Wallet hero** — estimated recovery + in-progress claims (best feature first)
2. **Settlement detail** — deadline, no-proof badge, mark claimed
3. **Private scan** — on-device email check
4. **Settlements browse** — searchable catalog
5. **Privacy promise** — no account, on-device notes

Never lead with a generic welcome/sign-up screen.

---

## ③ Momentum (reviews) — *dominate rankings*
Happy-moment prompt ships in `ReviewPrompt`: fires after the **2nd successful
claim/scan milestone**, once per version — never at launch or after an error.

---

## The flywheel
`keywords → impressions → authentic screenshots → happy moments → 5★ reviews →
algorithm rank-boost → win harder keywords → repeat.`

ASO is a **multi-year compounding asset.** Commit to the marathon.
